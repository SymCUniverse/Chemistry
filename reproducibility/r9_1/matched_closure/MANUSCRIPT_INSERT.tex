\subsection{Attempted End-to-End Matched-System Closure}
\label{sec:matched_closure}

To test whether the four layers defined here can already be closed on one
independently tracked chemical carrier, we preregistered a targeted search
requiring, under matched or quantitatively justified conditions:
(i) a mechanically licensed local carrier with independently determined
$\omega_0$ and damping $\gamma$;
(ii) modal/reaction-path information for that same carrier;
(iii) an independently established barrier together with the system's native
transmission/friction treatment; and
(iv) an observed kinetic, diffusion, or selectivity outcome not used to fit
the quantities in (i)--(iii).
Four candidates were frozen before final adjudication:
CO/Cu(001) surface diffusion, bridged/nonbridged
$\mathrm{Co_2(CO)_8}$ isomerization, IR-driven HONO isomerization in solid Kr,
and the independently parameterized generalized-Langevin description of
photoinduced electron transfer reported by Angulo \emph{et al.}
No candidate passed all admission gates.

CO/Cu(001) was the closest case.  The lateral frustrated-translation mode is
independently observed and damped, and independent electronic-structure and
spin-echo work support an activated bridge-saddle diffusion pathway.
However, the recovered damping records are not condition-matched closely
enough in temperature and coverage to the independent diffusion record, and
the experimentally documented rotational admixture of the frustrated
translation has not been reduced to a quantitative mass-weighted overlap with
the saddle direction.  We therefore classify this case as a near miss rather
than infer a fused rate relation.

The other three candidates fail for different reasons.  In
$\mathrm{Co_2(CO)_8}$, the reaction-coordinate friction mapping and basin
frequency used in the Kramers analysis are calibrated against the measured
rate series and therefore cannot serve as independent predictors of that same
series.  In HONO/Kr, loss of the initially pumped OH-stretch population shares
the reaction timescale with IVR and isomerization, while experiment and
full-dimensional theory show subsequent routing through torsional/internal
modes and the matrix cage; treating the total pumped-state decay as an
independent mechanical $\gamma$ would therefore be circular.  In the
photoinduced electron-transfer example, the solvent-polarization coordinate is
described by a non-Markovian memory kernel.  Its auxiliary-variable Markovian
embedding preserves that memory but does not license reduction to a single
local damping ratio.

Thus the present literature search yields no end-to-end empirical
$\chi$--$\mathsf{X}$--barrier--outcome closure satisfying the independence
criteria.  This negative result is retained rather than repaired by
substituting linewidth, viscosity, fitted friction, or reaction-population
decay for an independently licensed mechanical damping coordinate.  The
result does not imply that the four layers are unrelated; it defines the
measurement still required for a positive closure experiment.
