# Chemistry Project Guardrails v0.1

Status: ACTIVE PROJECT-LOCAL SAFEGUARDS  
Effective date: 14 September 2026  
Program authority: SymC General Operations Manual v0.8.0  
Scope: Chemistry-specific rules retained locally under the GOM migration-integrity principle.

This file does not replace frozen system protocols, release records, Atlas artifacts, or historical HOLDs. Where a frozen project artifact is stricter, that artifact governs its own lane. No scientific threshold or prior outcome is changed by this localization.

## 1. Native chemistry first

Chemical observables, electronic-structure models, kinetics, statistical mechanics, surface science, spectroscopy, and transport semantics are established before any SymC interpretation. A chi-related construction is withheld when the native dynamical model does not license it. Scalar, modal/vector, and conglomerate/system views are investigative starting components rather than an exhaustive decomposition.

## 2. Barrier-frequency firewall

Barrier-top frequency `omega_b` is a legitimate native quantity in barrier crossing, transition-state, Kramers, Grote-Hynes, instanton, and related kinetics when the source/model actually defines it.

`omega_b` must not be substituted for the restoring `omega_0` of the canonical second-order oscillator merely to construct mechanical chi. At an ordinary barrier saddle with anti-restoring curvature, the canonical restoring discriminant does not acquire a critical-damping point by notation alone. Therefore a ratio involving barrier frequency or friction does not inherit a `chi = 1` critical-damping or exceptional-point interpretation unless a separately licensed governing dynamical model demonstrates that boundary.

Kramers/Grote-Hynes friction, transmission coefficients, tunneling corrections, recrossing corrections, spectral broadening, electronic hybridization, and nuclear electronic friction remain distinct quantities unless the native derivation explicitly connects them.

## 3. Numerical settings cannot be selected by desired chemistry or SymC outcome

DFT slab thickness, vacuum, k-mesh, cutoffs, convergence tolerances, smearing, solver settings, and analogous numerical controls are selected and qualified using native numerical evidence. They may not be selected, rescued, or retuned because they improve agreement with rates, barriers, an Atlas coordinate, a chi value, a preferred mechanistic interpretation, or a desired downstream result.

Observed numerical failures and HOLDs remain historical facts. A successor numerical model may be qualified prospectively, but the successor does not rewrite the failed predecessor.

## 4. Substrate convergence and foundational inheritance

A minimum numerical PASS licenses only the action governed by that gate. When substantial downstream surface chemistry will inherit that substrate, GOM Section 11.3 applies: a bounded robustness challenge may be required before the substrate is treated as a heavily inherited foundation.

For H/Ru(0001), the fresh L17/V15/K24 successor fixed grid has passed its minimum four-case P0-Q qualification. Downstream relaxation is nevertheless held under `SYSTEM3_L17_FOUNDATIONAL_ROBUSTNESS_HOLD_v0.1.json` until the prospectively running L21 challenge resolves. The original PASS remains a PASS; the stronger hold governs inheritance, not retroactive adjudication.

A successful robustness challenge stops at its frozen stopping rule. There is no automatic L23/L25 or indefinitely deeper ladder. A contradictory challenge reopens only the materially dependent foundation and downstream stages.

## 5. Relaxation and adsorption inheritance

Clean-surface relaxation may proceed only from a substrate state authorized by the applicable numerical gate plus any active foundational-robustness dependency record. Hydrogen adsorption, site ordering, barrier searches, vibrational/modal analysis, and later stability-architecture interpretation may not inherit an unresolved substrate foundation as settled truth.

A local clean-surface PASS is not by itself a validated substrate-inheritance certificate. Certification remains governed by `architecture/VALIDATED_SUBSTRATE_INHERITANCE_CONTRACT_v0.2.md` or its explicitly versioned successor.

## 6. Dissipation and friction claim ceiling

A reaction rate, adsorption energy, electronic hybridization scale, linewidth, lifetime, or generic broadening term is not automatically a projected reaction-coordinate friction or damping coefficient.

For H/Ru(0001), the current literature audit status `DISSIPATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED` remains a Limit Map result. It blocks quantitative dissipation/chi claims requiring that coordinate but does not invalidate independently qualified upstream electronic-structure or clean-surface chemistry.

## 7. Quantum/path-integral claim ceiling

Architectural compatibility with QE plus i-PI, PIMD, instanton, or another quantum-nuclear route does not equal an executed or qualified production environment. Production quantum claims require versioned runtime qualification, reproducible inputs, convergence/uncertainty controls appropriate to the method, and the applicable project gate. Readiness documentation may support planning but may not be promoted into computed evidence.

## 8. Barrier Height/Rate Atlas firewall

Barrier Height/Rate Atlas v0.9 is a frozen retrospective independent evidence product. It remains read-only with respect to confirmatory System Model/Engine logic. It may not be silently retuned, rewritten, or used to choose upstream numerical settings.

Atlas coordinates retain their evidence grades, source relations, caveats, refusals, and independence ceilings. Model replicates do not inflate physical-coordinate counts. Calibration rows do not validate themselves. Proxies and reconstructed quantities retain their explicit status.

Any future extension that changes the frozen evidence product must be separately versioned rather than mutating v0.9 in place.

## 9. Independence and anti-circularity

Independence is tracked by pathway: raw data/source, system, outcome, tuning, method, Atlas, literature/source, and temporal pathway where applicable. The controlling question is whether shared information could force the agreement being presented as evidence.

Discovery and qualification evidence may inform a successor version, but it does not become untouched confirmation for that successor. Later rate agreement cannot rescue an upstream numerical failure. Later SymC agreement cannot validate a coordinate used to construct the same agreement.

## 10. Function Map and Limit Map

Chemistry work records both where a native representation functions and where it stops being licensed. Refusal, unresolved friction, nonconvergence, cross-phase mismatch, multichannel behavior, memory effects, and inability to define a coherent scalar are admissible scientific outputs rather than defects to conceal.

Extra computation after a claim-specific gate closes is labeled robustness, Function Map, Limit Map, sensitivity, or another accurate role. Such work may reopen an interpretation if materially contradictory, but it does not silently upgrade prior evidence.

## 11. Monitoring and recovery

Long-running electronic-structure work must preserve useful liveness/progress evidence and resumable checkpoints when scientifically and technically appropriate. Mechanical failures may receive bounded repair and restart. Numerical/scientific failures do not receive automatic threshold or model retuning.

A checkpoint is reused only after integrity and lineage validation. Completed valid endpoints are preserved rather than rerun solely because another endpoint failed mechanically. Repeated identical deterministic failures require diagnosis or circuit breaking rather than blind repetition.

## 12. Evidence and publication language

Every important Chemistry result carries the epistemic status it earned. P0-Q numerical qualification, retrospective Atlas agreement, literature-supported mechanism, prospective empirical confirmation, and method-scope validation remain distinct.

Manuscript, supplementary, repository, and tool prose must be resynchronized after capability or status changes. Smaller defensible claims are preferred over broader claims that rely on unresolved coordinates or inherited assumptions.

## 13. Historical preservation

The former `CHEMISTRY_GENERAL_PROTOCOL_v0.7.1A_ADOPTION_v0.1.json` remains preserved as historical governance lineage. GOM v0.8.0 adoption does not erase or relabel any historical HOLD, failed gate, exploratory result, qualification result, Atlas grade, or refusal.
